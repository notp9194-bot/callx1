# Instagram-style Reel share sheet

The Reels share sheet now has a richer, recipient-first flow:

- Shows a compact reel thumbnail, creator, and caption preview at the top.
- Adds instant contact search by name or UID.
- Supports selecting multiple recipients before sending.
- Shows a selected-recipient count and one-tap Send action.
- Keeps the existing Copy Link, external share, Story, Status, and Repost
  actions underneath.
- Keeps the sheet scrollable on smaller screens and preserves the existing
  single-recipient activity flow through the adapter's compatibility defaults.

No Gradle build or automated tests were run, as requested.
